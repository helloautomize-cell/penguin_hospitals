Why a penguin

Winter on the ice.

Penguin parents share one egg. One keeps it warm on their feet through the long, cold nights.

The other goes out to sea.

While one stays, the other walks to the water to find food. Neither can do both alone.

Then they swap.

The one who ate comes back and takes the egg. The one who waited finally goes to eat.

Neither does it alone.

That is the care we wanted to build. A team that takes turns staying with you, and a hospital that keeps the light on through the night. CONFIRM story wording

Questions

Asked at the front desk.

Who owns and runs Penguin Hospital?

The hospital is led by Dr. Lakshmi Vijay Kalbande, obstetrician and gynaecologist, together with Dr. Ashish A. Baviskar, cardiothoracic and vascular surgeon. CONFIRM ownership wording Both see patients themselves.

Is Penguin Hospital only for women?

No. Women's health and pregnancy are one half of what we do. The other half is heart, lung and vascular surgery for adults of any gender. Our emergency team sees anyone who needs urgent help.

Can my family stay with me?

One family member can usually stay with an admitted patient. In the labour room and theatre, the team will tell you when a companion can be present. CONFIRM visitor rules

Is the hospital open at night?

Yes. Emergency care is available day and night, including for women in labour. OPD consultations run at set hours, shown on the visit page.

Questions

Before you leave home.

How long does a first visit take?

Plan for about an hour, including registration, checks and any scan done the same day. Visits are longer if blood tests or a scan are needed. Follow-up visits are usually shorter.

Can I get my scan on the same day?

Often, yes. Our ultrasound room is next to the consultation room. Some scans need a full bladder or a specific week of pregnancy, and the team will tell you when.

Is there a pharmacy?

Yes. Penguin Pharmacy is part of the hospital, so you can collect your medicines before you leave. CONFIRM pharmacy hours

Can children visit an admitted patient?
