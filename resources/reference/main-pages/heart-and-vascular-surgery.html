What is the difference between a cardiologist and a heart surgeon?
A cardiologist diagnoses heart conditions and treats them with medicines and procedures through the blood vessels, such as angiography and stents. A cardiothoracic surgeon operates on the heart, lungs and major vessels, for example bypass or valve surgery. The two often work together to decide what is best.
I have been told I need heart surgery. Can I get a second opinion?
Yes, and it is sensible. Bring your angiography CD, echo report and other reports. Dr. Ashish will review them, explain what they show, and tell you whether surgery, a stent or medicines is the right option, and why.
Is heart surgery done at Penguin Hospital itself?
CONFIRM: which operations are done in-house and which at a partner hospital Dr. Ashish will tell you at your first visit where your operation would take place and who would be in the team.
Can women see Dr. Ashish?
Of course. Heart disease is the leading cause of death in Indian women too, and its symptoms in women can be less typical: tiredness, breathlessness, or pain in the back, jaw or stomach. Dr. Ashish sees men and women of all ages.
