About Penguin Hospital

A family hospital, named after the most devoted parents we know

Dr. Lakshmi's Penguin Hospital is a hospital in Chinchwad Gaon, Pimpri-Chinchwad, run by two specialists: Dr. Lakshmi Vijay Kalbande, an obstetrician and gynaecologist, and Dr. Ashish A. Baviskar, a heart and vascular surgeon. We look after women through pregnancy and beyond, and families through problems of the heart.

Book a visitSee the building
Why a penguin

Neither parent does it alone.

Penguin parents take turns. One keeps the egg warm through the winter while the other goes to find food, and then they swap. Neither does it alone. That is the kind of care we wanted to build: a team that stays with you, and a hospital that keeps the light on through the night. Our tagline says the same thing in three words: together through life. CONFIRM: client to approve or rewrite this story in their own words

How care works here

Four promises we keep on every visit.

i.

You see your specialist

Your visits are with Dr. Lakshmi or Dr. Ashish, not handed down a chain.

ii.

Explained in plain words

Before any test or operation, the doctor tells you what it is for, what it involves and what the choices are. Bring your family. Questions are welcome.

iii.

Under one roof

Consultation, ultrasound, the labour room, the operation theatre and the wards are in the same building, so you are not sent across the city between steps.

iv.

Open at night

The emergency team is here around the clock, and labour does not wait for office hours.

Quick answers

Questions patients ask

Did not find your question? Ask us on WhatsApp. Please do not send detailed medical information in a message.

Ask on WhatsApp
Who owns and runs Penguin Hospital?
The hospital is led by Dr. Lakshmi Vijay Kalbande, obstetrician and gynaecologist, together with Dr. Ashish A. Baviskar, cardiothoracic and vascular surgeon. CONFIRM: ownership wording Both see patients themselves, so the doctor you meet at your first visit is the doctor who looks after you through treatment and follow-up.
Is Penguin Hospital only for women?
No. Women's health and pregnancy are one half of what we do. The other half is heart, lung and vascular surgery for adults of any gender, led by Dr. Ashish Baviskar. Our emergency team sees anyone who needs urgent help.
Can my family stay with me?
One family member can usually stay with an admitted patient. In the labour room and theatre, the team will tell you when a companion can be present. CONFIRM: visitor rules
Is the hospital open at night?
Yes. Emergency care is available day and night, including for women in labour. Outpatient consultations run at set OPD hours, which are shown on our visit page.
