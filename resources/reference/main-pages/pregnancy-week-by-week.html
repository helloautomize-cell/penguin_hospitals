How the due date is worked out: we add 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of your last period. This assumes a 28-day cycle. Your dating scan in the first trimester gives a more accurate date, and Dr. Lakshmi will use that.

Quick answers

Questions about your due date

Did not find your question? Ask us on WhatsApp. Please do not send detailed medical information in a message.

Ask on WhatsApp
How accurate is a due date calculator?
It gives an estimate based on a 28-day cycle. Only about one baby in twenty is born on the due date itself; most arrive in the two weeks either side. If your cycles are longer, shorter or irregular, the dating scan between 8 and 13 weeks gives a more reliable date.
When should I have my first visit?
Book as soon as your home test is positive, ideally around 6 to 8 weeks. Come sooner if you have bleeding, strong one-sided pain, a past ectopic pregnancy, diabetes, high blood pressure or thyroid problems, or if you take regular medicines.
How many scans will I have?
Most healthy pregnancies have three or four: a dating scan, the NT scan around 11 to 14 weeks, the anomaly scan around 18 to 22 weeks, and sometimes a growth scan later. Dr. Lakshmi may advise more if your pregnancy needs closer watching.
When will I feel my baby move?
Most first-time mothers notice flutters between 18 and 22 weeks, and earlier in a second pregnancy. After about 28 weeks, learn your baby's usual pattern. If movements slow down or stop, come in the same day.
Is the week counted from conception?
No. Pregnancy weeks are counted from the first day of your last period, about two weeks before conception. So at "4 weeks pregnant", the baby has been growing for about two weeks.

Book your first pregnancy visit.

Ideally at 6 to 8 weeks. Bring the date of your last period and any reports.
