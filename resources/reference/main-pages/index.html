Quick answers

You asked. We answered.

Medical emergency? Call 108 or 112 first.
Is the hospital open on Sundays?
Penguin HospitalYes. The OPD runs on Sundays from 4 to 6 pm CONFIRM, and the emergency team is here all day and night.
Labour has started at 2 am. Can I come?
Penguin HospitalYes, come straight in. Call 95189 04156 on the way so the labour team is ready for you.
Is painless delivery available?
Penguin HospitalYes, with an epidural, after a talk with Dr. Lakshmi about whether it suits you and your labour.
Does every heart problem need surgery?
Penguin HospitalNo. Many heart conditions are managed with medicines and follow-up. Dr. Ashish explains when surgery would help, and what kind.
