Emergency care, day and night

Our emergency team is in the hospital around the clock, including for women in labour and for pregnancy emergencies. In a life-threatening emergency, call 108 or 112 for an ambulance first, then call us on +91 95189 04156 so we are ready when you arrive.

Do not wait

Come straight in, or call 108, if you notice:

These signs need a doctor now, not an appointment. If you are not sure, call us and ask.

In pregnancy

  • Bleeding from the vagina, at any stage
  • Water breaking, or a gush or steady leak of fluid
  • Regular, painful contractions before 37 weeks
  • Your baby moving less than usual, or not at all
  • A bad headache, blurred vision, or sudden swelling of the face and hands
  • Fits (seizures), fainting or severe tummy pain

After birth

  • Heavy bleeding that soaks a pad in an hour, or large clots
  • Fever, or a wound that is red, hot or leaking
  • Pain or swelling in one leg, or breathlessness

Heart and chest

  • Chest pain or pressure lasting more than a few minutes, especially with sweating, sickness, or pain in the arm, jaw or back
  • Sudden breathlessness
  • Fainting, or a very fast or irregular heartbeat
  • A cold, pale, painful leg

Anyone

  • Signs of stroke: face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech
  • Serious injury, heavy bleeding, or someone who cannot be woken
On the way

Three things to remember.

  1. Do not drive yourself if you have chest pain or are in strong labour.
  2. Bring your reports, pregnancy file and medicines, but do not delay to find them.
  3. If the person stops breathing, call 108 and start chest compressions if you know how.
What we can and cannot do

Honest about our limits.

We handle emergencies in pregnancy and birth, and heart, chest and vascular emergencies within our services. When a patient needs care we do not provide, such as major trauma CONFIRM, we stabilise them and arrange transfer to the right hospital.

Pregnancy, birth, heart, chest and vascular emergencies are handled here, day and night. For other emergencies we stabilise and arrange transfer.

Quick answers

Questions about emergencies

Did not find your question? Ask us on WhatsApp. Please do not send detailed medical information in a message.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do I need to call before coming in labour?
It helps. Call +91 95189 04156 on the way so the labour team is ready. But never delay coming in to get through on the phone. If labour is fast or there is bleeding, come straight in or call 108.
Is a doctor available at night?
Yes. A doctor is on duty at night, and the specialists are called in when you need them. CONFIRM: on-duty doctor arrangement
What if I think I am having a heart attack?
Call 108 or 112 for an ambulance straight away, then call us. Sit or lie down, loosen tight clothing and stay calm. Do not drive yourself, and do not wait to see if the pain passes. Take only the emergency medicine your doctor has already told you to take.
Do you have an ambulance?
CONFIRM For emergencies, 108 is the free ambulance service in Maharashtra.
