**H1: Your rights and responsibilities as a patient**

### Your rights
1. To be treated with respect and dignity, without discrimination.
2. To know the name and role of the doctors treating you.
3. To clear information about your condition, the proposed treatment, its risks and alternatives, in a language you understand.
4. To give or refuse consent to treatment, and to ask questions before deciding.
5. To privacy during examination, and to have a female attendant present during intimate examinations.
6. To confidentiality of your medical information.
7. To an estimate of costs before planned treatment, and an itemised bill.
8. To a copy of your medical records and discharge summary, on request, within the time the law allows.
9. To seek a second opinion.
10. To emergency care to stabilise you, regardless of ability to pay at that moment.
11. To raise a complaint and receive a response.

### Your responsibilities
1. Give complete and honest information about your health, past illnesses and medicines.
2. Follow the treatment plan you agreed to, or tell your doctor if you cannot.
3. Respect staff, other patients and hospital property.
4. Follow hospital rules on visitors, smoking and safety.
5. Settle bills as agreed, and tell us early about insurance.
6. Inform us if you cannot keep an appointment.
