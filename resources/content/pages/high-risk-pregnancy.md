**H1: High-risk pregnancy care**

A high-risk pregnancy is one where the mother or baby needs closer watching because of a health condition or a past experience. It does not mean something will go wrong. It means Dr. Lakshmi Kalbande builds a plan around your own risks, with more frequent visits and scans, and a team that is here day and night if you need us.

### Who it is for
A pregnancy may be called high-risk if you have:
- Twins or more
- High blood pressure, before or during pregnancy, or preeclampsia in a past pregnancy
- Diabetes, before or during pregnancy (gestational diabetes)
- Thyroid disease, heart disease, kidney disease, epilepsy or an autoimmune condition
- Two or more past miscarriages, a stillbirth or a premature birth
- A past caesarean, especially more than one
- Placenta previa (a low-lying placenta) or bleeding in pregnancy
- A baby that is growing too slowly or too quickly
- Age under 18 or over 35, or a pregnancy after IVF

### What happens at Penguin Hospital
- **A plan of your own.** At your first visit Dr. Lakshmi reviews your history and reports and writes down which visits, scans and tests you need, and when.
- **Closer watching.** More frequent blood pressure, sugar and growth checks. Dr. Lakshmi is trained in obstetric ultrasound and reviews many scans herself.
- **Working with other specialists.** If you have diabetes, a heart condition or a thyroid problem, we involve the right physician and share reports. [CONFIRM: visiting physicians]
- **Clear warning signs.** You go home knowing exactly what to watch for and when to come straight in.
- **A planned birth.** The timing and way of birth are discussed early, and changed if things change. The labour room and operation theatre are ready day and night.
- **Newborn readiness.** If your baby may need special care after birth, we plan for it in advance. [CONFIRM: NICU in-house or tie-up hospital]

### How to prepare
Bring every report from this and past pregnancies, discharge summaries, and a list of all medicines. If you test your sugar or blood pressure at home, bring your readings.

### After your visit
You leave with a written plan and our phone and WhatsApp number. Between visits, home readings can be shared on WhatsApp if Dr. Lakshmi asks for them. [CONFIRM]

**When to get help straight away.** Bleeding, leaking fluid, regular pains before 37 weeks, a bad headache, blurred vision or flashing lights, sudden swelling, pain under the ribs, fits, or your baby moving less than usual. Call 108 or 112, or come to our emergency entrance.

### FAQs
1. **Does a high-risk pregnancy mean I will need a caesarean?** Not necessarily. Many women with a high-risk pregnancy have a normal delivery. The way of birth depends on your condition, your baby's growth and position, and how labour progresses. Dr. Lakshmi discusses the options with you well before your due date, and again in labour.
2. **I have had two miscarriages. What can be done in my next pregnancy?** Before trying again, a visit to look for causes is useful. Tests may include blood tests and a scan of the womb. In the next pregnancy you will be seen early and often, with early scans for reassurance. Many women go on to have a healthy pregnancy.
3. **What is gestational diabetes?** It is high blood sugar that starts in pregnancy, found with a sugar test usually done between 24 and 28 weeks. Most women control it with diet and activity. Some need medicine. Good control lowers the risk of a very large baby and birth problems, and sugar usually returns to normal after birth.
4. **Can I have a normal delivery with twins?** Sometimes. It depends mainly on how the first baby is lying, whether the babies share a placenta, and your own health. Twins are often delivered a little earlier than single babies. Dr. Lakshmi will explain the safest plan for your twins as the pregnancy progresses.
5. **I am over 35. Is my pregnancy high-risk?** Age alone is one factor. Women over 35 have a slightly higher chance of blood pressure problems, diabetes and some chromosomal conditions, so screening and checks matter. Most women over 35 have healthy pregnancies. Dr. Lakshmi will tailor your visits to your overall health, not just your age.
