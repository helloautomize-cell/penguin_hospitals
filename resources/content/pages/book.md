**H1: Book a visit**

Choose your doctor and a day that suits you, and we will call or WhatsApp you to confirm the time. If you would rather talk now, call +91 95189 04156.

Emergency banner: This form is not for emergencies. If you or someone with you is very unwell, call 108 or 112, or come straight to our emergency entrance.

**Form** (master prompt 9.1): patient name · mobile · age (years or months) · doctor (Dr. Lakshmi Kalbande / Dr. Ashish Baviskar / Not sure) · reason (Pregnancy check-up / High-risk pregnancy / Period problems or PCOS / Fibroids or cysts / Fertility / Menopause / Surgery opinion / Heart check-up / Heart surgery opinion / Varicose veins or leg arteries / Other) · preferred date (today to 60 days) · time of day · city (optional) · under-18 checkbox with guardian fields · consent checkbox · consent line.

Side cards:
- **What happens next.** We confirm your slot by call or WhatsApp, usually within a few working hours [CONFIRM]. You get the date, time and doctor in writing.
- **What to bring.** Previous reports and scans, a list of your medicines, your ID and insurance card if you have one. If you are pregnant, bring your pregnancy file.
- **Sunday visits.** The OPD runs on Sunday from 4 pm to 6 pm [CONFIRM]. Emergency care is open every day and night.
