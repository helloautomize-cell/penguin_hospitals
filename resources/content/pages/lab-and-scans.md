**H1: Lab tests and scans**

Many tests you need are done in the same building as your consultation, so you can often see your doctor, have a scan and give blood samples in one visit. [CONFIRM: which tests are in-house]

### Ultrasound
Our ultrasound room is next to the consultation room.
- **Pregnancy scans:** dating scan, NT scan, anomaly scan, growth and Doppler scans [CONFIRM each]
- **Gynaecology scans:** womb, ovaries, follicle tracking for fertility
- **Other scans:** [CONFIRM: abdominal, venous and arterial Doppler]

Dr. Lakshmi Kalbande is trained in gynaecological and obstetric ultrasound and reviews many scans herself. Sex determination of the foetus is a crime under the PCPNDT Act; we do not disclose the sex of the baby.

### Heart tests
ECG, 2D echo and treadmill test (TMT) [CONFIRM in-house].

### Preparing for tests
- **Fasting tests:** no food for 8 to 12 hours; water is fine.
- **Early pregnancy and pelvic scans:** some need a full bladder; drink water an hour before.
- **TMT:** comfortable clothes and walking shoes.

### FAQs
1. **Can I get reports on WhatsApp?** [CONFIRM] Many patients prefer digital reports. Your doctor will still go through them with you, because numbers on a report need context.
2. **Do I need an appointment for a scan?** Book scans in advance, especially pregnancy scans that must be done in a certain week. Some scans are done the same day during your consultation.
3. **Why can't you tell me the baby's sex?** Revealing the sex of a foetus is illegal in India under the PCPNDT Act, to prevent sex-selective abortion. Doctors and centres who reveal it face severe penalties. We do not disclose it in any way, including by hints.
