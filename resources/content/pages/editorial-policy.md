**H1: How we write and review our content**

Every medical page on this website is written in plain language and reviewed by the doctor responsible for that service before it is published.

### Who reviews
- Women's health and pregnancy pages: Dr. Lakshmi Vijay Kalbande, MBBS, DNB (Obstetrics and Gynaecology)
- Heart, lung and vascular pages: Dr. Ashish A. Baviskar, MBBS, MS, MCh (CTVS)
Each page shows the reviewer and the date it was last reviewed.

### Our standards
- We explain what a condition is, who it affects, and what happens at our hospital.
- We follow current Indian and international guidance where it exists.
- We do not make claims of cure, guarantees, comparisons with other hospitals, or claims we cannot support.
- We do not publish patient testimonials, success rates or prices, in line with National Medical Commission rules.
- We do not name medicine brands.

### AI-assisted content and images
We use AI tools to help draft text and to create illustrative images. Every page is checked, edited and approved by our doctors before publishing. AI-generated images are used to illustrate topics, and never to show real patients, real results or our facilities. Photos of our hospital rooms are real.

### Updates and corrections
Pages are reviewed at least once a year, or sooner when guidance changes. If you spot an error, write to [email] and we will check and correct it.
