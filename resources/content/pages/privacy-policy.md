**H1: Privacy policy**

Last updated: [date]

This policy explains how [CONFIRM legal entity] ("Penguin Hospital", "we") handles personal data collected through penguinhospitals.com, WhatsApp, phone and our forms, under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). It does not cover your medical records at the hospital, which are governed by medical record rules and kept confidential under professional regulations.

### 1. Who we are
[Legal entity name], Plot No. 59, New Pawana Nagar Society, Bijli Nagar Road, Chinchwad Gaon, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Maharashtra 411033. Contact for privacy: [CONFIRM name, email, phone] (Grievance Officer).

### 2. What we collect
- **When you book or contact us:** name, mobile number, age, the doctor and reason you choose, preferred date, city, and, for minors, a guardian's name.
- **When you message us on WhatsApp or call:** your number and what you tell us.
- **When you visit the website:** basic technical data (device type, pages visited) only if you accept analytics cookies.
We ask you not to send detailed medical information through website forms.

### 3. Why we use it
- To arrange and confirm appointments
- To answer your questions and call you back
- To send reminders you have asked for
- To improve the website, if you allow analytics
We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

### 4. Legal basis
We process your data with your consent, given when you tick the consent box or contact us. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to the Grievance Officer. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done.

### 5. Who we share it with
- Our hospital staff who need it to help you
- Service providers who run our website, email and forms, under contract and only for these purposes [CONFIRM: hosting, email provider]
- Authorities, when the law requires it

### 6. How long we keep it
Appointment request data is kept for [CONFIRM, e.g. 12 months] and then deleted, unless it becomes part of your medical record.

### 7. Your rights under the DPDP Act
You may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, nominate a person to act for you, and raise a grievance. Write to the Grievance Officer. We will reply within [CONFIRM: 30 days]. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

### 8. Children
For anyone under 18, we need a parent or guardian's consent before processing their data.

### 9. Cookies and analytics
We use only essential cookies by default. Analytics (Google Analytics) loads only if you click Accept on our cookie banner. Maps and videos load only when you click them. You can change your choice at any time from the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.

### 10. Security
We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), limit staff access, and choose providers with sound security practices. No system is completely secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

### 11. Changes
We will post any changes here with a new date.
