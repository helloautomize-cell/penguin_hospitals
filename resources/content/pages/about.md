**H1: A family hospital, named after the most devoted parents we know**

Dr. Lakshmi's Penguin Hospital is a hospital in Chinchwad Gaon, Pimpri-Chinchwad, run by two specialists: Dr. Lakshmi Vijay Kalbande, an obstetrician and gynaecologist, and Dr. Ashish A. Baviskar, a heart and vascular surgeon. We look after women through pregnancy and beyond, and families through problems of the heart.

### Why a penguin
Penguin parents take turns. One keeps the egg warm through the winter while the other goes to find food, and then they swap. Neither does it alone. That is the kind of care we wanted to build: a team that stays with you, and a hospital that keeps the light on through the night. Our tagline says the same thing in three words: together through life. [CONFIRM: client to approve or rewrite this story in their own words]

### How care works here
- **You see your specialist.** Your visits are with Dr. Lakshmi or Dr. Ashish, not handed down a chain.
- **Explained in plain words.** Before any test or operation, the doctor tells you what it is for, what it involves and what the choices are. Bring your family. Questions are welcome.
- **Under one roof.** Consultation, ultrasound, the labour room, the operation theatre and the wards are in the same building, so you are not sent across the city between steps.
- **Open at night.** The emergency team is here around the clock, and labour does not wait for office hours.

### What the building holds
- **Labour room** with a newborn warming station, so your baby is checked and kept warm the moment they are born.
- **Operation theatre** for caesarean births, laparoscopic surgery and other planned and emergency operations.
- **Ultrasound room** next to the consultation room, for pregnancy scans and gynaecology scans.

2. **Is Penguin Hospital only for women?** No. Women's health and pregnancy are one half of what we do. The other half is heart, lung and vascular surgery for adults of any gender, led by Dr. Ashish Baviskar. Our emergency team sees anyone who needs urgent help.
3. **Can my family stay with me?** One family member can usually stay with an admitted patient. In the labour room and theatre, the team will tell you when a companion can be present. [CONFIRM visitor rules]
