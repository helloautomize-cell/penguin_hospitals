**H1: Nutrition and physiotherapy**

Food and movement are part of treatment, not extras. At Penguin Hospital, [CONFIRM: dietitian and physiotherapist names or visiting arrangement] help you with eating and exercise plans linked to your care.

### Nutrition
- **Pregnancy:** gaining the right amount of weight, managing nausea, and diet for diabetes in pregnancy
- **PCOS:** meals that support weight and sugar control
- **Heart health:** less salt, healthier fats, and Indian meals that suit a heart diet
- **After surgery:** protein for healing

### Physiotherapy
- **In pregnancy:** back pain, posture and preparing for labour
- **After birth:** pelvic floor exercises and safe return to exercise
- **After heart and lung surgery:** breathing exercises, walking plans and cardiac rehabilitation
- **After gynaecology surgery:** getting moving safely

### FAQs
1. **Do I need a special diet in pregnancy?** You need a balanced diet with enough protein, iron and calcium, not extra fat or sugar. Most women need only about 300 extra calories a day from the second trimester. A dietitian helps if you have diabetes, are underweight or overweight, or have severe nausea.
2. **When can I start pelvic floor exercises after birth?** Usually within a few days after a normal birth, gently. After a caesarean, start when comfortable. Regular pelvic floor exercises help prevent leaking urine and support recovery.
