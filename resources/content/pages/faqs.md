**Visiting and booking**
- *What are your OPD hours?* Monday to Saturday [CONFIRM], Sunday 4 pm to 6 pm [CONFIRM]. Emergency care is open day and night, every day. Each doctor's OPD timings are shown on their profile and confirmed when you book.
- *How do I book?* Use the online form, call +91 95189 04156, or message us on WhatsApp. We confirm your slot by call or message. Tell us the reason for your visit so we give you the right doctor and enough time.

**Pregnancy and birth**
- *Can I choose painless delivery?* Most women in labour can have an epidural for pain relief, if there is no medical reason against it. Dr. Lakshmi discusses it at your antenatal visits, and the anaesthetist checks you before it is given. You can decide during labour too.
- *Can my husband be with me during delivery?* [CONFIRM labour companion policy] Many women find a companion helpful, and we will tell you in advance what is possible in the labour room and theatre.
- *Do you tell the baby's sex on the scan?* No. Sex determination of the foetus is a crime under the PCPNDT Act, and we do not disclose it in any way.
- *Is a paediatrician present at birth?* [CONFIRM] Every newborn is checked straight after birth, and kept warm on the newborn warming station in the labour room.

**Heart and vessels**
- *I have been told I need bypass surgery. Can I get a second opinion?* Yes. Bring your angiography CD and reports to Dr. Ashish. He will go through them with you, explain the options, including whether stenting or medicines could be enough, and tell you plainly what he recommends.
- *How long is the hospital stay after heart surgery?* It varies with the operation and your recovery. Many patients go home within a week after bypass or valve surgery, and sometimes sooner after keyhole surgery. Dr. Ashish will give you an expected plan before the operation.
