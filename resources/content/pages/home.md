### Heart map
H2: Tap a part of the heart. See how Dr. Ashish treats it.
Lede: A plain-language map of heart surgery. Every operation starts with tests and a conversation, and many heart problems never need surgery at all.
1. **Coronary arteries.** When the heart's own arteries are narrowed or blocked, bypass surgery (CABG) carries blood around the blockage, either on the heart-lung machine or on a beating heart.
2. **Heart valves.** A valve that is too tight or leaks can be repaired, or replaced with a tissue or mechanical valve.
3. **The aorta.** The body's main artery can weaken and widen. Surgery replaces the weak section before it causes harm.
4. **Keyhole access (MICS).** For some patients, surgery is done through a small cut between the ribs instead of opening the breastbone. Many go home sooner.
5. **Lungs and chest.** Surgery for lung growths, fluid or air around the lungs, and chest injuries.
Beyond the heart: Varicose veins · Leg artery disease · AV fistula for dialysis
