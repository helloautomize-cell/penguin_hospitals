**H1: Medical disclaimer**

The information on this website is written to help you understand health conditions and the care we offer. It is reviewed by our doctors, but it is general information. It cannot take into account your own history, examination and tests.

- Do not start, stop or change any treatment based on this website. Talk to a doctor.

- Tools such as the due-date calculator and pregnancy week guide give estimates only.
- Images on this site may include illustrations and AI-generated images used to explain topics. They do not show our patients. See our editorial policy.
- In an emergency, call 108 or 112.
