**H1: Accessibility**

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or voice control.

### What we have done
- Aimed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, and India's GIGW guidelines where they apply
- Text of at least 17 pixels, good colour contrast, and pages that work when zoomed to 200 percent
- Every image has a text description
- Every page can be used with a keyboard alone
- Animations stop if your device is set to reduce motion
- Large tap targets on mobile, and Call and WhatsApp buttons always within reach

### Known limits
Some older documents and third-party content, such as maps, may not be fully accessible. Ask us and we will give you the information another way.
