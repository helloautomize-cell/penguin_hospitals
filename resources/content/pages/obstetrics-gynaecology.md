**H1: Obstetrics and gynaecology in Chinchwad**

Obstetrics is care in pregnancy and birth. Gynaecology is care for a woman's reproductive health at every other time, from the first period to the years after menopause. At Penguin Hospital both are led by Dr. Lakshmi Kalbande, with the ultrasound room, labour room and operation theatre in the same building.

### Pregnancy and birth (glass cards)
- **Pregnancy care.** Check-ups, scans and tests from the first visit to the birth.
- **High-risk pregnancy.** Closer care for twins, diabetes, high blood pressure, or a past loss.
- **Preeclampsia.** Watching blood pressure and acting early.
- **Normal delivery.** Support for a natural birth when it is safe.
- **Painless delivery.** Epidural pain relief in labour.
- **Caesarean delivery.** Planned or emergency, when it is the safer way.
- **After birth.** Recovery, feeding, stitches and your six-week check.

### Gynaecology (glass cards)
- **PCOS.** Irregular periods, acne, weight and fertility, explained and managed.
- **Irregular periods.** Finding the reason your cycle has changed.
- **Heavy periods.** Bleeding that runs your month.
- **Fibroids.** Growths in the womb that may or may not need treatment.
- **Ovarian cysts.** Most are harmless. Some need a closer look.
- **Endometriosis.** Painful periods are not something to just live with.
- **Menopause care.** Hot flushes, sleep, bones and the heart.
- **Laparoscopic surgery.** Keyhole and hysteroscopic surgery.
- **Fertility evaluation.** The first tests when a pregnancy is taking time.

### What your care includes (step tracker)
1. **Book.** Online, by phone or WhatsApp.
2. **Talk.** Dr. Lakshmi listens to your history and concerns, with your family if you wish.
3. **Examine and scan.** Often in the same visit, in the ultrasound room next door.
4. **Your plan.** Written down: what is happening, what to do, what to watch for.
5. **Treatment.** Medicines, a procedure, or surgery when needed.
6. **Follow-up.** In person or on WhatsApp for simple questions. [CONFIRM]

### FAQs
1. **Do I need to be pregnant to see an obstetrician-gynaecologist?** No. Dr. Lakshmi sees women at every age for periods, pain, discharge, PCOS, fibroids, cysts, contraception advice, screening and menopause. Pregnancy is one part of her work. You can book for any women's health concern, and you do not need a referral.
2. **At what age should a girl first see a gynaecologist?** There is no fixed age. A visit makes sense if periods have not started by 15, if they are very painful, very heavy or very irregular, or if there are signs such as excess hair or acne that worry the family. A mother can come with her daughter.
3. **Is an internal examination always done?** No. Many visits need only a talk, an abdominal examination and an ultrasound. If an internal examination would help, Dr. Lakshmi explains why and asks your permission first. You can ask for a female attendant to be present.
4. **Can I get a scan on the same day as my consultation?** Often, yes. Our ultrasound room is next to the consultation room, and Dr. Lakshmi is trained in gynaecological and obstetric ultrasound. Some scans need a full bladder or a particular week of pregnancy, and we will tell you when.
