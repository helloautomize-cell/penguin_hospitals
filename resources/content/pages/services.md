**H1: Our services**

Penguin Hospital has two main departments, each led by its specialist, and an emergency team that is here day and night.

**Women and pregnancy, with Dr. Lakshmi Kalbande.** Pregnancy care, high-risk pregnancy, preeclampsia, normal, painless and caesarean delivery, postnatal care, PCOS, period problems, fibroids, ovarian cysts, endometriosis, menopause, laparoscopic and hysteroscopic surgery, fertility evaluation. [Go to women and pregnancy]

**Heart and vessels, with Dr. Ashish Baviskar.** Heart check-up, keyhole heart surgery, bypass surgery, valve surgery, lung and chest surgery, varicose veins, leg artery disease, AV fistula for dialysis. [Go to heart and vessels]

**Emergency, day and night.** Labour and pregnancy emergencies, chest pain and heart emergencies. [Emergency care]

**Also at the hospital** (shown only when confirmed): newborn and child care, lab and scans, nutrition and physiotherapy.
