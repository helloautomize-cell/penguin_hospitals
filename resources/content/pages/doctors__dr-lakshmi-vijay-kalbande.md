### About Dr. Lakshmi
Dr. Lakshmi Kalbande is the obstetrician and gynaecologist who founded Penguin Hospital, and the "Lakshmi" in its name. She looks after women from their first period problems through pregnancy, birth and menopause, and she performs laparoscopic (keyhole) and hysteroscopic surgery.

Her training includes a DNB in obstetrics and gynaecology and a fellowship in gynaecological and obstetric ultrasound, so she reads many scans herself and can explain what they show during the same visit. Before founding Penguin Hospital she worked as a consultant gynaecologist at Amrutha Multi-specialty Hospital in Telangana.

She has a particular interest in high-risk pregnancy: twins, diabetes or high blood pressure in pregnancy, placenta previa, and women who have lost a pregnancy before. Each of these pregnancies gets its own plan, with closer visits and a clear sense of what to watch for at home.

### How Dr. Lakshmi works
- **Your pregnancy, your plan.** Visits and scans are scheduled around your own risks, not a generic chart.
- **Normal birth first, when it is safe.** She discusses normal and painless delivery with every mother, and recommends a caesarean only when it is the safer way for mother or baby.
- **Keyhole surgery when it is the right step.** Many gynaecology problems settle with simpler treatment first. When surgery is needed, she offers laparoscopy where it suits you.
- **Questions are part of the visit.** Bring your list. Bring your husband or mother. Nothing is decided until you understand it.
