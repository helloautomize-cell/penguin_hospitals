### About Dr. Ashish
Dr. Ashish Baviskar is a cardiothoracic and vascular surgeon: a surgeon for the heart, the lungs and the blood vessels. He has worked for more than 15 years in hospitals in several Indian cities before joining Penguin Hospital.

His work covers coronary artery bypass surgery (CABG), heart valve repair and replacement, surgery of the aorta, lung and chest surgery, and vascular surgery for the legs and for dialysis access. He performs minimally invasive cardiac surgery (MICS) for suitable patients, reaching the heart through a small cut between the ribs.

Many patients come to him worried that they will need an operation. Often the first step is simply tests and a clear explanation. When surgery is the right choice, he walks the patient and their family through what will happen, before and after, in plain words.

### How Dr. Ashish works
- **Tests and a conversation first.** No decision is made until the reports are in and you have understood them.
- **Keyhole when it suits you.** He offers MICS where your heart condition and body allow it, and explains honestly when open surgery is safer.
- **Your family is in the room.** Heart surgery affects the whole household. Bring them to the consultation.
- **Recovery is planned too.** You leave with a clear plan for medicines, wound care, walking and follow-up visits.

### FAQs
1. **What does a cardiothoracic and vascular surgeon do?** A CTVS surgeon operates on the heart, the lungs and the blood vessels. That includes bypass and valve surgery, lung surgery, and operations on the arteries and veins of the legs. If you have been told you may need heart or vessel surgery, this is the specialist who decides whether and how.
2. **Do I need a referral to see Dr. Ashish?** No. You can book directly. If you already have ECG, echo or angiography reports, or a letter from your cardiologist or family doctor, bring them so the first visit is as useful as possible.
3. **Does every heart problem need surgery?** No. Many heart conditions are managed with medicines and follow-up, or with procedures done by a cardiologist. Dr. Ashish recommends surgery only when the tests show that it is the right option for you.
4. **Is keyhole heart surgery possible for everyone?** No. It depends on the heart problem, your body shape, your lungs and your other health conditions. Dr. Ashish will tell you after reviewing your reports whether MICS is suitable, or whether open surgery is safer for you.
