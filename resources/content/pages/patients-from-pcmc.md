**H1: Coming to us from across Pimpri-Chinchwad**

Families come to Penguin Hospital in Chinchwad Gaon from Pimpri, Nigdi, Akurdi, Ravet, Moshi, Wakad, Hinjawadi, Punawale and Tathawade. Here is how to make each trip count, especially in pregnancy, when you may visit many times.

### Make one trip do more
- **Book ahead.** A fixed slot means you are not waiting with a long drive home ahead.
- **Scan and consultation together.** Our ultrasound room is next to the consultation room, so many scans happen in the same visit.
- **Bring everything.** Old reports save repeat tests.
- **Follow up on WhatsApp.** Many questions between visits can be answered on WhatsApp. [CONFIRM]

### In labour from a distance
Leave earlier than you think. For a first baby, set off when contractions are regular and about 5 minutes apart, or straight away if your water breaks, you see bleeding or your baby is moving less. For a second baby, labour is often faster, so leave sooner. Call us on the way.

### Travel notes
From Wakad, Hinjawadi, Punawale and Tathawade, allow extra time at peak hours. From Pimpri, Akurdi and Nigdi, head towards Chinchwad Gaon and Bijli Nagar Road. [CONFIRM: typical drive times, nearest bus stops]
