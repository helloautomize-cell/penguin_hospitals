**H1: Plan your visit**

A little planning makes your visit shorter and calmer. Here is what to bring, when to come, and what happens if you are admitted.

### Before you come
- Book a slot online, by phone or on WhatsApp. Walk-ins are seen during OPD hours, but a booking saves you waiting.
- Bring every report and scan you have, even old ones, and a list of the medicines you take.
- Pregnant? Bring your pregnancy file and your partner or a family member if you can.
- Some blood tests need you to fast. If you are not sure, ask us on WhatsApp the day before.

### When you arrive
1. Register at the front desk with your name, mobile number and ID.
2. A nurse checks your weight, blood pressure and pulse.
3. You see your doctor. Scans that are needed are often done in the ultrasound room next door.
4. You leave with a written plan: medicines, tests and your next visit.

### If you are admitted
- **Admission:** the desk explains the room options and an estimate of costs before admission. [CONFIRM room types]
- **What to pack:** loose comfortable clothes, slippers, toiletries, your medicines in their boxes, phone charger. For delivery: clothes, caps and socks for the baby, and maternity pads.
- **Companion:** one family member can usually stay with you. [CONFIRM]
- **Discharge:** you get a discharge summary, your medicines and a follow-up date. Keep the summary safe; your future doctors will ask for it.

### Insurance and payment
Many patients use health insurance with us. Bring your policy card, photo ID and any pre-authorisation forms. Our desk will tell you before admission whether your policy is cashless here or needs reimbursement. [CONFIRM: TPA and insurer panel list, payment modes]

### Getting here
By road from Pimpri, Akurdi or Nigdi, head for Chinchwad Gaon and Bijli Nagar Road. We are opposite Laxmi Medico and Mart. [CONFIRM parking, lift and wheelchair access]

### FAQs
1. **How long does a first visit take?** Plan for about an hour, including registration, checks and any scan done the same day. Visits are longer if blood tests or a scan are needed. Follow-up visits are usually shorter.
2. **Can I get my scan on the same day?** Often, yes. Our ultrasound room is next to the consultation room, so many pregnancy and gynaecology scans are done during the same visit. Some scans need a full bladder or a specific week of pregnancy, and the team will tell you when.
3. **Is there a pharmacy?** [CONFIRM] If not in-house, Laxmi Medico is directly opposite the hospital.
4. **Can children visit an admitted patient?** [CONFIRM visiting rules] To protect newborns and patients after surgery, we may limit visitors at times.
