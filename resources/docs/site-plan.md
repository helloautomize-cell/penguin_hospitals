# Penguin Hospital: site plan

Status: draft for approval, 1 November 2026
Brand: Dr. Lakshmi's Penguin Hospital ("Penguin Hospital" in running copy) · Chinchwad Gaon, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Pune
Domain: penguinhospitals.com (stays) · URLs end with a trailing slash

**Total: 53 pages, plus 2 launch blog posts.** That is 12 core pages, 19 for women and pregnancy (the hub plus 18), 9 for heart and vessels (the hub plus 8), 3 other services, 7 legal pages, the health library, and 2 utility pages. The two hubs aren't numbered in the tables below.

Pages marked **[CONFIRM]** are built, but they stay hidden until the client confirms that the service really exists, along with who provides it. That way nothing on the site claims a service the hospital doesn't offer.

## 10. Home page, top to bottom (approved design)

1. Photo film hero: three chapters (a new life, a strong heart, every night), a chapter reel and a live Chinchwad clock
2. Every chapter under one roof: three photo cards
3. A life in chapters: pinned horizontal story (growing up, nine months, first breath, the heart)
4. For mothers-to-be: pregnancy week dial and due-date calculator, linking to the full tool page
5. Heart map: tap a part of the heart and see how Dr. Ashish treats it
6. Meet your doctors: Dr. Lakshmi and Dr. Ashish
7. Visit: 24-hour opening dial, address card and areas served
8. Quick answers: chat-style FAQ, with the emergency line on top
9. Closing band and footer

No testimonials, ratings, patient counts, prices or "best" claims anywhere (Indian medical advertising rules).

3. Pacemaker: who performs it, or leave it off.
4. Pap smear and colposcopy, heart tests and laser for veins: done in-house, yes or no.
