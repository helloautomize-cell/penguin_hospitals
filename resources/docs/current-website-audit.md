# Penguin Hospital: current website audit

Site: https://penguinhospitals.com/ · Crawled: 1 November 2026 (all 14 URLs in the sitemap)
Source: the live site only. No WordPress export, screenshots or Google Business Profile access yet. Anything not on the site is marked [CONFIRM].

## 0. Executive summary

- **What it is:** a multi-specialty hospital in Chinchwad Gaon, PCMC (Pimpri-Chinchwad), Pune. Two named doctors: an obstetrician-gynaecologist (Dr. Lakshmi Vijay Kalbande) and a cardiothoracic and vascular surgeon (Dr. Ashish A. Baviskar). Tagline "Together through Life!".
- **Content:** 12 live content pages. Six long gynaecology condition pages (about 1,500 to 2,300 words each) were added in August 2026 and read as SEO-written. Services, About and Contact are thin. Cardiology and cardiac surgery have no pages at all, even though the homepage leads with them.
- **Biggest problems:**
  1. 🔴 **Compliance.** Patient testimonials and a Google reviews embed on the homepage, "best" claims on at least 6 pages, unsourced patient and delivery counts, price tables on every condition page, drug names, and "permanent cure" wording. All of these break the Indian medical advertising rules (master prompt Part 6.3).
  2. **Half the hospital is invisible.** Dr. Baviskar has no profile page and cardiac surgery (MICS, CABG, valves, vascular, varicose veins) has no page, although the reviews are mostly about him.
  3. **Trust gaps.** No registration number for Dr. Baviskar, no council name for Dr. Kalbande, no legal pages, a Gmail address, ©2024 footer.
  4. **Technical SEO.** No true H1 on most pages, demo post still in the sitemap, an orphan duplicate page, a "#" menu link, en_GB locale.

## 9. Issues

### 🔴 Live problems (compliance)
1. Patient testimonials and Google review quotes on the homepage (not allowed).
2. "Best" claims: "best high risk pregnancy treatment in PCMC", "best female gynaecologist in Pune", "best maternity hospital in PCMC", "best hospital for fibroids", "best hospital for irregular menstruation", and "best hospital for preeclampsia" pasted onto the PCOS page.
3. Boast numbers: 10,000+ patients, 5,000+ deliveries, 3,000+ laparoscopies, 2,000+ open-heart surgeries, 1,000+ MICS.
4. Price tables on 7 pages (consultation ₹600 to ₹1,500; delivery packages ₹35,000 to ₹1,40,000; surgery up to ₹1,50,000).
5. Drug names (metformin, letrozole, clomiphene, cabergoline, Mirena, tranexamic acid, GnRH analogues).
6. Cure and outcome claims: hysterectomy as a "permanent cure"; irregular periods "resolved"; "vast majority" healthy outcomes; "within months".
7. No privacy policy or any legal page; contact form has no consent notice (DPDP).
8. No registration number for Dr. Baviskar; no council named for Dr. Kalbande.

### Positioning
- Heart surgery is the hospital's rarest skill in PCMC and has no page.
- Two strong, distinct specialties ("women and heart") are not framed as one story.

### Conversion
- WhatsApp is the only booking path; no callback form, no per-doctor timings.
- No "what to expect at your first visit", no emergency strip.

### Technical SEO
- No true H1 on most pages (main heading is H2).
- Demo post and ?page_id=5 in the post sitemap; /thank-you/ indexable.
- Orphan /obstetrics-care/ duplicates /obstetrics-gynaecology/.
- en_GB locale; truncated meta descriptions; ©2024 footer.
- Copy errors: unfinished FAQ answer (travel), duplicated step text, "we develops", "Does Dr. Lakshmi Kalbande performs", UK leftovers ("10p coin").

### Local SEO
- NAP is consistent apart from phone formats and two map links.
- Areas-served list exists; no area or "coming from outside" page; no LocalBusiness/Hospital schema verified.
